Validation

TEST

The designs were reviewed through usability testing and internal feedback to identify areas of friction, validate the workflow and refine the experience before handoff.

What We Validated

Workflow Clarity

Could users understand the overall Time Off setup journey and what they needed to complete at each stage?

Information & Configuration

Could users review, understand and update policy information without becoming overwhelmed by the configuration details?

Interaction & Guidance

Could users identify the required actions, understand system guidance and confidently move through the setup process?

Feedback

Feedback indicated that users needed a quicker way to navigate between policies and identify the specific policy requiring attention.

Design Outcome

The validation process helped refine the information structure, interactions and guidance so the final experience could support complex policy setup while keeping the workflow understandable and manageable.
