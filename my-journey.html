KEY PROJECTS

Smart Implementation

AI-assisted implementation experience that simplified onboarding, reduced duplicate client interactions and accelerated module configuration.

Product Area
Enterprise HCM | Implementation Experience
Project Overview
Designed an AI-assisted enterprise implementation experience focused on simplifying complex client onboarding and configuration workflows. The experience brought relevant implementation information and AI-generated insights and recommendations into a centralized Data Hub, helping implementation teams and clients complete module configuration with fewer client interactions.
Key Responsibilities
  • Led UX strategy and end-to-end product design from problem definition and discovery through design validation and delivery.
  • Partnered with Product, Architecture, Engineering, QA, SMEs and business stakeholders to understand implementation challenges, define requirements and evaluate design feasibility.
  • Analyzed existing onboarding and configuration workflows to identify opportunities to reduce duplicate client interactions.
  • Designed user flows, information architecture, interaction patterns, wireframes, prototypes and high-fidelity UI.
  • Designed AI-assisted experiences that surfaced insights and recommendations within the implementation workflow.
  • Structured the experience around a centralized Data Hub to help users access relevant implementation information.
  • Focused on making recommendations understandable and actionable while keeping users involved in reviewing and confirming decisions.
  • Considered usability, accessibility, design consistency and technical feasibility throughout the design process.
  • Collaborated with engineering and other cross-functional teams to ensure the intended experience could be implemented effectively.
Outcome
The redesigned experience reduced duplicate client interactions and shortened enterprise onboarding timelines, enabling module configurations to be completed within one or two client meetings.
